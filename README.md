# Ansys-Simulation-of-Naca0012

**🛩️ CFD Simulation of NACA 0012 Airfoil – ANSYS Fluent**

**Description:**

Performed Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) simulations on a NACA 0012 airfoil at 0° and 5° angles of attack using ANSYS Fluent to evaluate aerodynamic performance. The goal was to determine lift and drag coefficients and visualize key flow characteristics around the airfoil.

**Key Features:**

**1.** Simulated 2D incompressible flow over NACA 0012 airfoil

**2.** Generated and analyzed velocity, pressure, and streamline contour plots

**3.** Compared aerodynamic behaviour at different angles to study lift generation and flow separation.

**Tools & Technologies:**
ANSYS Fluent, 
CFD, 
NACA Airfoil, 
Lift & Drag Analysis, 
Post-Processing.
